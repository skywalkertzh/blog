About Me

1 min · 213 words · Zenghao Tang |  Suggest Changes

I am currently a senior at the Global College (GC), formerly known as the University of Michigan-Shanghai Jiao Tong University Joint Institute (UM-SJTU JI), majoring in Electrical and Computer Engineering.

In terms of academic performance, I was awarded the National Scholarship and achieved a perfect 4.0 GPA during the 2023-2024 academic year, ranking first in my major (1/233). In terms of research, I have a strong focus on embodied AI and humanoid robotics. I began my research journey at the APEX Lab (SJTU) under the supervision of Prof. Weinan Zhang, exploring sim-to-real transfer and natural gait generation. Following this, during the Spring of 2025, I was an exchange student and research assistant at the LeCAR Lab at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Guanya Shi. Currently, I am also a research intern at the Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, advised by Prof. Weinan Zhang and Dr. Jingbo Wang.
