Retargeting Matters: General Motion Retargeting for Humanoid Motion Tracking

1. Why:#

These humanoid motion tracking policies are a fundamental tool for building teleoperation pipelines or hierarchical control systems. However, significant differences still exist between humans and humanoids in terms of bone length, joint range of motion, kinematic structure, body shape, mass distribution, and actuation mechanisms. This embodiment gap is the first major hurdle that must be overcome for 3D human motion data to be fully useful for humanoid learning.

2. Evaluation标准:#

Q1. Does the choice of retargeting method impact the performance of the motion tracking policies?
Q2. What retargeting artifacts negatively impact the policy and prevent it from being able to learn?
Q3. To what extent do different retargeting methods preserve the “look” of the source motion?

Metrics:#

The paper evaluates the policies based on balance maintenance and tracking performance:

  • Success Rate: Defined as the ratio of successful rollouts to the total number of rollouts. A rollout is considered successful if the robot reaches the end of the episode without its anchor body height or orientation deviating significantly from the reference (beyond a given threshold).
  • Tracking Performance:
    • $E_{g-mpbpe}$ (mm): The average position error of body parts in global coordinates.
    • $E_{mpbpe}$ (mm): The average position error of body parts relative to the root position.
    • $E_{mpjpe}$ ($10^{-3}$ rad): The average angular error of joint rotations.

3. 现有的retargetting方法:#

  • PHC:
    • SMPL model: 接受人体的形状参数和姿态参数 (由形状参数和姿态参数驱动) return一个有姿态的人体网格Vh。为了得到关节点的位置q，还需要用一个关节回归器J，从网格Vh中计算出 3D 关节位置j
    • SMPL格式的运动文件: body的shape参数和一个pose param的随时间变化的tensor
    • 然后把机器人的形状参数代替人的姿态参数，放入SMPL model，再用关节回归器J，得到每个关节的位置target P。
    • 机器人的根部位置pt 机器人的朝向Rt 机器人的各个关节角度qt 通过正向kinematics计算之后来减小和target P之间的误差
    • 接着用梯度下降对所有帧数进行优化
  • ProtoMotions:
    • 这是一个用于训练运动模仿策略的标准实现包，自带基于优化的重定向算法。
    • 缩放: 使用自定义缩放因子对源动作的世界坐标轴进行缩放。
    • 优化: 使用 Mink 最小化缩放后的人体与机器人关键部位之间的关节位置和方向误差。
    • 后处理: 包含高度调整步骤，将最低高度设置为源动作中的最低高度（而非设为0）。

  • GMR (Proposed Method):
    • 动机: 旨在修复其他方法（如PHC, ProtoMotions/Mink）产生的明显伪影，例如与源动作的偏差、滑步、地面穿透和自相交。
    • 核心差异: 改进了源动作的缩放处理方式，这通常是伪影的主要来源。
    • 流程: 包含两阶段优化 (Two-stage optimization) 来求解机器人的动作。
